Jollof Rice

A delicious West African dish loved by many.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups rice
  • Tomatoes
  • Onions
  • Spices
  • Chicken or fish

Preparation Steps

  1. Wash the rice
  2. Prepare the tomato sauce
  3. Cook the rice with sauce
  4. Simmer until done
